## Features

### Rain you can watch
A small pump lifts water into the cloud, and it falls back as real drops — slow and scattered on Drizzle, steady on Steady, a proper shower on Pour. It’s the part people stop and stare at.

### Light that stays soft
Seven colors, from warm amber and candlelight to sea glass and midnight blue, plus a slow color cycle. Three brightness levels, with the lowest dim enough to leave on all night without lighting up the room.

### A fine, cool mist
An ultrasonic plate turns a little of the water into a cool mist that drifts up through the cloud. Two levels — Low and Standard — for the air right around your desk or bedside.

### Quiet by design
A rated pump hum of 32 dB(A) or less, a felt coaster to take the edge off hard surfaces, and an auto-off timer (4, 6 or 8 hours) so it winds down on its own.

### One cable, no fuss
USB-C power, four tactile buttons, and a 300 ml tank that lifts off in one piece for filling and cleaning. When the water runs low, the pump and mist stop automatically. The light stays on.

## How it works

- **Eyebrow:** How it works
- **Headline:** Make it rain, indoors.

1. **Fill the basin.** Lift the cloud, pour in up to 300 ml of distilled or filtered water, and set the cloud back on its stem.
2. **Plug in and pick your weather.** Connect USB-C, then tap Rain to choose Drizzle, Steady or Pour. Tap Mist for Low or Standard.
3. **Set the glow.** Tap Light to move through seven colors and the slow cycle; hold it to dim. Tap Timer for 4, 6 or 8 hours — or leave it running and let the low-water shut-off handle the rest.

**What’s happening inside:** The rain recirculates. A small pump in the base draws water up through the stem into the cloud, where it drips back through tiny holes into the basin. Separately, an ultrasonic plate vibrates fast enough to turn a little water into mist. Only the mist uses water up, which is why one fill lasts a full evening.

## Care

- **Eyebrow:** Care
- **Headline:** A minute a day keeps it clear.

Any device that holds open water needs a little care. Mistlo is designed to make that quick: the cloud lifts off, the basin is open and smooth, and a soft brush for the drip holes is in the box.

- **Daily:** Unplug, empty the basin, rinse, and let the parts air-dry with the cloud off.
- **Weekly:** Soak the cloud top and basin in equal parts white vinegar and water for 20–30 minutes, brush the drip holes, rinse well.
- **Always:** Use distilled or filtered water, never add oils, and never submerge the base.

Follow our [2-minute cleaning & care guide](/guides/how-to-clean-rain-cloud-humidifier/) for the full routine, including how to clear a slow drip hole without scratching the plastic.
