faq:
  - q: "What is Mistlo?"
    a: "Mistlo is a tabletop rain cloud humidifier and night light. A small matte cloud rains into its own basin, releases a fine cool mist and glows in seven soft colors. It’s designed for a desk, nightstand or reading nook. See the full details on the [Mistlo product page](/)."
  - q: "How big is the tank, and how long does one fill last?"
    a: "The tank holds 300 ml. That’s up to 8 hours of mist on Standard and about 12 hours on Low. The rain recirculates — drops fall back into the basin and are pumped up again — so rain-only mode uses very little water."
  - q: "Will Mistlo humidify my whole room?"
    a: "No. Mistlo’s mist output is about 20–30 ml per hour, which is right for your personal space — the area around a desk or bedside — but not enough to change the humidity of a whole room. For that, a multi-liter room humidifier is the better tool."
  - q: "What kind of water should I use?"
    a: "Distilled or filtered water is best. Tap water works, but its minerals clog the drip holes faster and can leave a fine white dust nearby. Use fresh water each time rather than topping up. Our [water and oil guide](/guides/distilled-water-and-essential-oils/) explains why."
  - q: "Can I add essential oils?"
    a: "No — Mistlo is water only. Oils can coat the mist plate, clog the drip holes and soften plastic over time. If you’d like scent, place a reed diffuser or scent stone nearby."
  - q: "How loud is Mistlo?"
    a: "The pump and mist are rated at 32 dB(A) or less at 1 meter — a low hum. The drip sound depends on the rain speed (Drizzle, Steady or Pour) and the surface underneath. The included felt coaster softens it on hard desks."
  - q: "Can I use the light without water?"
    a: "Yes. The light runs independently of the rain and mist and works with an empty tank. Choose from 7 colors or a slow color cycle, at 3 brightness levels. Mistlo remembers your last color."
  - q: "How is it powered?"
    a: "Mistlo uses USB-C (5V ⎓ 2A input, about 6 W max). A 1.5 m USB-C to USB-C cable is included; the wall adapter isn’t. Any USB-C wall adapter rated 5V ⎓ 2A or higher will work. Laptop ports may not supply enough power for the pump and mist together."
  - q: "How do I clean it?"
    a: "Unplug it, empty the basin and let the parts air-dry daily. Once a week, soak the cloud top and basin in equal parts white vinegar and water for 20–30 minutes, brush the drip holes with the included brush and rinse well. Never submerge the base. Follow our [step-by-step cleaning guide](/guides/how-to-clean-rain-cloud-humidifier/) for the full routine."
  - q: "Can I leave it running overnight?"
    a: "Mistlo has an auto-off timer (4, 6 or 8 hours) and stops the pump and mist automatically when the water runs low, leaving the light on. Place it on a stable, level surface away from the edge, keep the cable tidy, and keep it out of reach of small children and pets."
  - q: "What’s in the box?"
    a: "The Mistlo cloud and base, a 1.5 m USB-C to USB-C cable, a felt coaster, a soft cleaning brush for the drip holes, and a quick-start and care card."
