**Quick answer:** If your rain cloud humidifier has stopped dripping, check four things in order: the water is above the minimum line, there’s no air bubble trapped in the pump, the drip holes aren’t clogged with scale, and the cloud top is seated correctly. Most units are back to normal after a top-up, a tilt, or a 30-minute vinegar soak.

Before you start, **unplug the unit** any time you put your hands in the water or move parts around. Then work through the table and fixes below.

## Quick Diagnostic Table (Symptom → Likely Cause → First Fix)

| Symptom | Likely cause | First fix |
|---|---|---|
| No rain at all, pump silent | No power, low-water shut-off, rain switched off | Check cable, adapter and water level (Fix 1, 7) |
| No rain, pump humming | Air lock in pump or tube | Tilt and re-prime (Fix 2) |
| Only one or two drips | Clogged drip holes | Vinegar soak and brush (Fix 3) |
| Rain stops after a few minutes | Water level borderline; air drawn in | Fill to the max line (Fix 1) |
| Drips only on one side | Cloud not level or seated | Re-seat and level (Fix 4) |
| Water splashing on the desk | Overfilled, speed too high, unit tilted | Fix 5 |
| Light works, no mist | Mist off, float stuck, plate scaled | Fix 6 |
| Buzzing or rattling | Low water, debris, weak power, resonant surface | Fix 7 |

## Fix 1–2: Check Water Level & Clear Air Locks in the Tube

### Fix 1: Check the water level

Most rain cloud units have a **minimum and maximum line** in the basin. Below the minimum, two things happen: the low-water sensor cuts the pump and mist, and the pump intake starts sucking air. Rain will sputter or stop.

- Fill with fresh water to just below the max line.
- Don’t overfill — water above the max line can splash or reach parts it shouldn’t.
- Remember that mist uses water up over the evening, so a unit that rained fine at 8 p.m. may stop at midnight. That’s the shut-off doing its job.

### Fix 2: Clear an air lock

An air bubble trapped in the pump or the tube can stop water reaching the cloud even when the pump is running. You’ll often hear a hum with no rain.

1. Unplug the unit.
2. Make sure the water is at the max line.
3. Gently tilt the base side to side a few times to let the bubble escape from the pump.
4. If the tube is accessible, lift the cloud off and check the tube is fully seated and not kinked.
5. Plug back in and run the rain on its fastest setting for 30 seconds to push water through, then turn it back down.

## Fix 3–4: Unclog Mineral-Blocked Drip Holes & Check Top Alignment

### Fix 3: Unclog the drip holes

This is the most common long-term cause, especially if you use hard tap water. Minerals build up in the tiny holes until only one or two still drip.

1. Unplug, lift off the cloud top and empty it.
2. Soak it for 20–30 minutes in equal parts white vinegar and water.
3. Brush the holes gently from inside and outside with a soft brush.
4. Rinse under a running tap so water flows through the holes.
5. For stubborn holes, use a soft plastic pick. **Avoid metal pins**, which can scratch or enlarge holes and cause uneven, splashy drops.

For the full routine, including the mist plate and basin, follow our [weekly vinegar cleaning steps](/guides/how-to-clean-rain-cloud-humidifier/).

### Fix 4: Check the cloud is seated and level

If the cloud sits slightly crooked on the stem, water pools on one side of the cloud reservoir. You’ll get rain from one side only — or none, if water is leaking back down the outside of the stem instead.

- Lift the cloud off and seat it back firmly and straight.
- Check any rubber seal or gasket where the tube meets the cloud is in place and not twisted.
- Make sure the whole unit sits on a flat, level surface.

## Fix 5: Stop Water From Splashing Outside the Basin

Splashing is usually a setup issue, not a fault.

- **Lower the rain speed.** Fast settings drop more water from height. The slowest setting is the least splashy.
- **Don’t overfill.** A brim-full basin turns every drop into a splash.
- **Center the cloud** over the basin (see Fix 4).
- **Level the surface.** Even a slight tilt sends drops toward the rim.
- **Clear partial clogs.** When some holes are blocked, the remaining ones carry more water and drip faster and harder.
- **Protect the surface.** A coaster or small tray under the base catches stray drops and protects wood finishes.

Never let water pool around the power port. If water gets into the base, unplug it and let it dry fully before use.

## Fix 6: Mist Plate Stopped Working (Gentle Swab Reset)

The light running while the mist doesn’t is a common combination, because the LED is powered separately.

1. **Check the mist is on.** On many units, the mist button cycles through levels and off.
2. **Check the water level.** Most ultrasonic plates need water within a certain depth — too low and the shut-off triggers; too high and some plates struggle to break the surface.
3. **Check the float.** Many units have a small floating sensor in the basin that tells the device there’s water. If it’s stuck down with scale or debris, the unit thinks it’s empty. Free it gently with your finger.
4. **Clean the plate.** Unplug, empty, then gently wipe the small round disc at the bottom of the basin with a cotton swab dipped in diluted white vinegar. Rinse with a damp swab. Don’t scrape or press hard — the plate is delicate.
5. **Let it dry** before refilling and testing.

## Fix 7: Quiet a Buzzing Pump or Check USB-C Power Output (5V/2A)

### If the pump buzzes, rattles or gurgles

- **Top up the water.** A pump pulling in air is noisy.
- **Clear the intake.** With the unit unplugged, check for hair, lint or debris near the pump inlet and remove it.
- **Re-prime** using the tilt method in Fix 2.
- **Move it** off glass or hollow wood, or put a felt pad underneath. Resonance can make a normal hum sound like a buzz.

### If it’s weak, flickering or cutting out

Most rain cloud humidifiers need a USB power source that supplies **5V at 2A (10 W)**. Laptop ports, old phone chargers and some multi-port hubs supply less, especially when other devices are connected. Symptoms include weak rain, mist that comes and goes, or a light that flickers when the pump starts.

- Plug into a USB wall adapter rated 5V ⎓ 2A or higher.
- Try a different cable — damaged or very long cables can drop voltage.
- Check the port on the unit is dry and free of lint.

### When to stop troubleshooting

If the pump is completely silent with good power and the right water level, or if you see any sign of water inside the electronics, scorching or a burning smell, **unplug it and stop using it.** Don’t open a sealed base or attempt electrical repairs. Contact the manufacturer or replace the unit.

## Prevent It Next Time

Most dripping problems come back to two things: minerals and standing water. Using distilled or filtered water and emptying the unit daily prevents the majority of clogs. Our guide to [avoiding mineral and oil buildup](/guides/distilled-water-and-essential-oils/) explains why.
