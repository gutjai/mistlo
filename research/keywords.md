> **Methodology & Estimates Notice:** All demand ratings, competition guesses, and ranking timelines below are **estimates** derived from live SERP composition, Amazon/Reddit buyer discussions, and Google autocomplete patterns (no paid keyword APIs used, per `PROJECT.md` free-tools-only rule).

## Strategy for a Brand-New Free Subdomain (`*.pages.dev` / `*.github.io`)

A new subdomain with zero backlinks will **not** rank on page 1 for head terms like `"humidifier"` or `"rain cloud humidifier"` in weeks. Instead, we win fast with a **three-tier funnel**:
1. **Tier 1 (Fastest Wins — 2–6 weeks):** Specific troubleshooting, cleaning, water-type, and sound/noise long-tail queries where forums and thin dropship sites dominate.
2. **Tier 2 (Mid-Term — 4–10 weeks):** Buyer-evaluation queries (`do rain cloud humidifiers work`, `rain cloud vs regular humidifier`) and niche gift queries (`aesthetic gifts under $50`).
3. **Tier 3 (Anchor Commercial Terms — 2–4+ months):** Product-page commercial keywords supported by internal links from all 7 guides and `/faq/`.
