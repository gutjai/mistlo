# Product Name & Brand Check

## 1. "Drizzle" Clash Assessment

"Drizzle" is **not safe** as our brand name. Web search checks show overlap both in our exact product category and across high-authority brands that would bury a brand-new subdomain in search results.

| Conflict Area | Findings | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| **Same-category generic use** | Retailers and marketplace listings (Amazon, Dick Smith, Woolworths, eBay) already use "drizzle" descriptively in titles for rain-cloud / water-drip humidifiers. | High |
| **Home fragrance / diffusers** | Existing reed diffuser products use "Drizzle" in product names (e.g., Meraki *Verbena Drizzle*, French Connection *Lemon Drizzle*, Yorkshire Candle Co.). | High |
| **Lighting overlap** | Luxury lighting fixtures include "Light Drizzle" naming (e.g., Ochre *Light Drizzle Chandelier*). | Medium |
| **Unrelated SERP giants** | **Drizzle ORM** (major TypeScript SQL library), **Graza Drizzle** (viral olive oil), and **Drizzle Honey** (B-Corp food brand) dominate branded queries. | High |

**Verdict:** Replace "Drizzle" with a clean, invented DTC brand name that owns its branded SERP on day one and avoids impersonating or colliding with any real company.
