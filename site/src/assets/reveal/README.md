# Reveal media

- Replace `face.jpg` with a photograph of the owner that you have permission to publish. Use a square crop around 800 × 800 pixels and aim for under 150 KB. The included image is an illustrated placeholder.
- Optionally add `reveal.mp4`. The photo will become the video poster. Use a square or landscape video with H.264 video and AAC sound, about 10–20 seconds at 720p, ideally under 3 MB. Include captions in the video if there is dialogue. Playback starts when the visitor presses Play. The written reveal is always visible.
- Rebuild after changing either file.
